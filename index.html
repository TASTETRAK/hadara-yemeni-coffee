Our Story

A name that carries a civilization.حضارة

Long before coffee reached Europe, it was roasted, brewed and shared in the highlands of Yemen and shipped to the world through the port of Mokha. Every cup of Yemeni coffee carries that history: slow extraction, warm spice, and the habit of sitting down together.

Hadara brings that tradition home to Stockton. For years the 209 drove to the Bay for a proper cup of Yemeni coffee. We built this house on West Lane from the ground up so you don't have to: a place for long talks, late study sessions and good company, with coffee that is sustainably sourced and made with passion.

The Coffee

A field guide to Yemeni coffee.

New to Yemeni coffee? Swipe through the classic styles of a Yemeni coffee house, then order ahead to see everything Hadara is pouring today.

قشرi.

Qishr

A light, golden brew made from the dried husk of the coffee cherry, simmered with ginger and spice. The oldest cup in Yemen.

Ginger · Husk · Warm spice
شاي عدنيii.

Adeni Chai

Named for the port city of Aden: black tea simmered with milk, cardamom and spice until it turns rich and silky.

Cardamom · Milk · Black tea
مفوّرiii.

Mufawar

Yemeni coffee brewed slowly with milk for a deep, smooth cup. The house favorite at Yemeni cafés everywhere.

Slow-brewed · Creamy · Bold
جبانيiv.

Jubani

Light-roast Yemeni coffee brewed together with the husk, cardamom, ginger and cinnamon. Fragrant and bright.

Light roast · Cinnamon · Ginger
صنعانيv.

Sana'ani

Named for the old city of Sana'a: black Yemeni coffee with cardamom. Bold, aromatic and unsweetened unless you ask.

Black · Cardamom · Bold
